Instructions

Part 1: Using it

It looks and acts like a normal Google image search.

1
Open the app. It starts on Google's home page with a blank search bar.
2
Tap "IMAGES" at the top left, next to "ALL". The app opens on ALL with Google's trending searches. Switch to IMAGES before you search, just like on real Google.
If you forget and search from ALL, it's fine. As soon as you search for a word containing "card" or "cards", the app switches to IMAGES by itself and shows the photo grid.
3
Search for anything that contains the word "card" or "cards". Tap the search bar, type it, and tap search. Any of these work:
card · cards · red playing cards · what's my card? · deck of cards
A search without the word "card" shows no photos. It says "Your search did not match any images", as Google does for a search with no results. Just search again with the word "card" in it.
4
Find your trigger card. By default it is set to the 1st image. You can change its spot in Settings (Trigger image number).
5
Tap that picture. After the delay you chose in Settings (the small dots count down one per second if you turned them on), the card lifts off the screen and grows to full size.
6
Move it around. Drag it with your finger or flick it so it bounces around your screen. It automatically settles at a natural angle so you can slide it off the screen with your thumb or finger. If you choose "Card vanishes when you lift your finger" in Settings, it disappears the moment you let go.
7
Phone Motions. Turn on Enable motion and Shake disconnect in Settings, then shake the phone and the card falls away.
8
Blow release (optional). Turn on Blow release in Settings and tap Allow when the phone asks to use the microphone. On the photo grid, blow toward the bottom of the phone. The trigger image jitters like it is caught in the wind. Blow a second time and it jitters, then after 3 seconds the card comes out of its frame and grows to full size. From there it works as in steps 6 and 7. An orange dot shows at the top of the screen while the microphone is on.

Practice and reset

↻
Double tap anywhere to put the folded card back on the page, ready to practice again, without starting the countdown.
↻
Tap the Google logo at the top to start over from the blank home page.
Open Settings any time with the small gear in the top left corner of the home page. Use "Card size" if the card on screen does not match the real one.

Part 2: Add it to your iPhone Home Screen

Do this once. The app then opens full screen, with no Safari bars, like a real app. Use Safari, not another browser.

1
Open the app's web address in Safari. The address is the one you were given (it ends in .netlify.app).
2
Tap the Share button. It is a square with an arrow pointing up, in Safari's bottom bar. If you do not see it, tap the three dots first.
or
3
Scroll down the list and tap "Add to Home Screen".
Add to Home Screen
4
Check the name, it should say DG Card. If you see an "Open as Web App" switch, leave it turned on.
5
Tap "Add" in the top right corner.
Add
6
Open it from your Home Screen. Find the new DG Card icon and tap it. From now on always start the app from this icon.
Tip: if the bottom of the screen looks cut off or the bar sits too high, open Settings and use "Lower the fake bottom bar" until it sits at the very bottom, and make sure "Scroll the whole page" is ticked.
Updated app? Delete the Home Screen icon and add it again so the phone loads the newest version.
