Instructions

Part 1: Using it

It looks and acts like a normal Google image search.

1
Open the app. It starts on Google's home page with a blank search bar.
2
Tap "IMAGES" at the top left, next to "ALL". The app opens on ALL with Google's trending searches. Switch to IMAGES before you search, just like on real Google.
If you forget and search from ALL, it's fine. As soon as you search for a word containing "card" or "cards", the app switches to IMAGES by itself and shows the photo grid.
3
Search for anything that contains the word "card" or "cards". Tap the search bar, type it, and tap search. Any of these work:
card · cards · red playing cards · what's my card? · deck of cards
A search without the word "card" shows no photos. It says "Your search did not match any images", as Google does for a search with no results. Just search again with the word "card" in it.
4
Find your trigger card. By default it is set to the 1st image. You can change its spot in Settings (under "Trigger Card Location", the box called "Which picture is it?").
5
Tap that picture. After a short delay (5 seconds unless you change it in Settings), the card lifts off the screen and grows to full size. Small dots at the top edge of the screen count down one per second. In Settings you can move them just above the bottom bar or turn them off.
Prefer not to tap? Turn on "Start by touching and scrolling" in Settings. Then touching the picture and starting to scroll begins the countdown. Tapping still works too.
6
Move it around. Drag it with your finger or flick it so it bounces around your screen. It automatically settles at a natural angle so you can slide it off the screen with your thumb or finger.
Prefer not to slide the card off? Turn on "Card vanishes when you let go" in Settings. Then the card disappears the moment you let go.
7
Phone Motions (optional). Turn on Card reacts to phone tilt in Settings to allow the card to react to your phone's movement. Turn on Shake to let the card fall and the card will pop out of its frame when you shake your phone.
8
Blow mode (optional). In Settings, under Performance Modes, turn on Blow mode to give the illusion that you are causing the card to react to you blowing on your screen. Tap the trigger image as usual. When the countdown ends, the card flutters and jitters like an old sticker coming unstuck in the wind. Settings lets you choose up to 3 flutters (the last one always comes loose) and how long the pause between them lasts. Between flutters the countdown dots run again to help with your timing. When it comes loose, it grows to full size and moves around the screen. From there it works as in steps 6 and 7.
9
Card Name Mode (optional). In Settings, under Performance Modes, turn on Card Name Mode. Tap the search bar and type the name of the card, for example "queen of hearts". Spelling does not matter, and the app fixes it. The search bar only ever shows "what is my card?" as you type. The phrase keeps pace with your typing, so it is complete by the time you finish. Pressing search always completes it. Press search. When the photos appear, the caption under the trigger image shows the card you typed. A second switch in Settings adds the word "Signed" before the card name in that caption. Turn the mode off to search normally.
10
Money Mode (optional). In Settings, under Performance Modes, turn on Money Mode. Now search for money or coins instead of "card". The grid fills with money pictures, and your trigger picture is a folded $20 bill or a coin, whichever you pick in Settings. Everything else works the same way. Turn Money Mode off to go back to cards. Money Mode and Card Name Mode can't be on at the same time.

Practice and reset

↻
Double tap anywhere to put the folded card back on the page, ready to practice again, without starting the countdown.
↻
Tap the Google logo at the top to start over from the blank home page.
Open Settings any time with the small gear in the top left corner of the home page. If the card on screen does not match the real one in size, tap "Advanced Settings" at the bottom of Settings and use "Card size".

Part 2: Add it to your iPhone Home Screen

Do this once. The app then opens full screen, with no Safari bars, like a real app. Use Safari, not another browser.

1
Open the app's web address in Safari. The address is the one you were given.
2
Tap the Share button. It is a square with an arrow pointing up, in Safari's bottom bar. If you do not see it, tap the three dots first.
or
3
Scroll down the list and tap "Add to Home Screen".
Add to Home Screen
4
Check the name, it should say DG Card. If you see an "Open as Web App" switch, leave it turned on.
5
Tap "Add" in the top right corner.
Add
6
Open it from your Home Screen. Find the new DG Card icon and tap it. From now on always start the app from this icon.
Tip: if the bottom of the screen looks cut off or the bar sits too high, open Settings and scroll to the bottom and tap "Advanced Settings". Use "Lower the bottom bar" until it sits at the very bottom, and make sure "Scroll the whole page" is turned on.
Updated app? Delete the Home Screen icon and add it again so the phone loads the newest version.
